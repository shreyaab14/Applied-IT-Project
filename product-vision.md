Product Vision Statement - CCTV checker

FOR (target customer): Security teams, building managers, and organizations that use CCTV cameras to keep people and places safe.

WHO (statement of the need or opportunity): Often find it difficult to monitor multiple camera feeds at the same time. Important activities or possible safety risks can be missed, especially when monitoring footage for long periods.

THE (product name) IS A (product category): CCTV Checker AI is an AI-based video monitoring system that analyses activities captured by security cameras.

THAT (key benefit, compelling reason to use): Helps security teams notice unusual activities and possible safety risks more quickly by highlighting important moments in CCTV footage for further review.

UNLIKE (primary competitive alternative): Traditional CCTV systems, where security staff often have to watch camera feeds themselves or rely on basic motion-detection alerts.

OUR PRODUCT (statement of primary differentiation):CCTV Checker AI aims to go beyond basic motion detection by analysing activity patterns and identifying predefined situations that may need attention. Instead of trying to judge a person's intentions, it focuses on observable activities and alerts human operators when something may require further investigation. The goal is to reduce manual monitoring effort while keeping people responsible for the final decisions.
